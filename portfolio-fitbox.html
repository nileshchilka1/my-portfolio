FitBox – AI Fitness Trainer

Project information

  • Project duration: Aug 2022 – Nov 2022
  • Collected data for various exercises and poses like Pushups, Plank, Situps, Squats, Chair Pose, etc.
  • Used MediaPipe Python Library for Landmarks Prediction of the body.
  • Based on X, Y Coordinates and Angles, classified the pose and action of the body and exposed as WebRTC.
  • Built Flutter based APP for Frontend and used Firebase for storing data and integrated Stripe for Payments.

Tech: OpenCV, MediaPipe, Pillow, WebRTC, gTTS, Stripe, Flutter, Android.
