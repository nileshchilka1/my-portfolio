MagicMirror – AI Photo Clicker

Project information

  • Project duration: Sep 2025 - Sep 2025
  • Implemented “Wave” & “Like” gesture recognition using Mediapipe to Click & Download Photo respectively.
  • Used Nano Banana for Enhancing overall image of the guests and Pose Enhancement.
  • Built QR Code to scan & Download Photos of Guests from FastAPI & deployed on Raspberry PI 4.
